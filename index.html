    It's all about Operation

    • locals for Mahanoy Plane, Maizevill and Gilberton
    • 3 staging tracks down the center of the peninsula
    • a train from staging is brought to the arrival tracks
    • cars are classified for the locals
    • cars returned by locals are put on the "outbound" track
    • outbound train returns to staging
    • the engine returning to staging is put on the train waiting in staging
    • MT hoppers in the yard can be swapped with hoppers at the breaker
